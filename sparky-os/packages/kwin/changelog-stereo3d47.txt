kwin (4:6.7.4-2+stereo3d47) unstable;

  * Carry three fixes of KWin's Plasma/6.7 branch that our 6.7.4-based
    source lacks, each upstream's own commit with its authors (ORDER 449):
    - Vlad Zahorodnii's 5f587fb72b of 6.7.6 (BUG 510116),
      SurfaceInterface::removeExtension also removes the extension's
      state from the current surface state, so a destroyed and a new
      surface extension at the same address no longer give
      SurfaceState::mergeInto() a state of the wrong type, a KWin crash
      (s47-0001).
    - Xaver Hugl's 37d327ce46 of 6.7.6, with Rafał Domecki, the
      occlusion pass of WorkspaceScene skips hidden items, so a closed
      but cached effect view (QuickSceneEffect) no longer culls every
      window's damage on its output, and windows go on repainting
      (s47-0002).
    - Vlad Zahorodnii's 0de99deddb of 6.7.5, with zhang shoucheng, the
      xdg_popup's parent surface is a QPointer, so a client that destroys
      the parent wl_surface first and then repositions the popup no
      longer reaches a freed pointer (s47-0003).
    The headers of these patches and of the published s41-0001 (Vlad
    Zahorodnii's 0ea31ae7) keep the authors' names and the invent commit URL
    and no longer their email addresses. Stereo KWin's own code is
    untouched. The edition adds an autotest of its own for the first (s47-0004, testSurfaceExtensionInterface): a surface
    extension recreated at the address of a destroyed one of another type
    gets a state of its own type.
