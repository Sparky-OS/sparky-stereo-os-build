kwin (4:6.7.4-2+stereo3d49) unstable;

  * A configure that changes only a window's states keeps the size the
    client committed. XdgToplevelWindow::sendRoleConfigure() fills every
    configure with the move-resize geometry, and while a configure was
    scheduled the committed geometry was ignored, so a client that resized
    itself when its popup closed and the window was activated again got the
    old size back, acknowledged it and lost its resize. Only a configure that
    asks for a size (maximize, fullscreen, tiling, an interactive resize or
    any other resize by KWin, recorded by moveResizeInternal(); a move after
    the request does not clear it) now holds the committed geometry back
    (s49-0001, ORDER 459). Tests: testXdgShellWindow's
    testSelfResizeWhileActivationConfigureIsPending,
    testMaximizeWinsOverSelfResizeBeforeConfigure and
    testResizeThenMoveKeepsRequestedSizeBeforeConfigure (s49-0002).
