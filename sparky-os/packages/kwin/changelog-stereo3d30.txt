kwin (4:6.7.4-2+stereo3d30) unstable; urgency=medium

  * The fork's branches are one again: Sparky-OS/kwin stereo3d30 is
    stereo3d (HDMI 3D structures, L + depth, output protocols at version
    28, X11 child windows) with paired-output-recovery rebased on it, the
    code this package already carried in +stereo3d29.
  * The DMA-BUF format switch test commits its buffers without asking for
    a frame callback and drops them instead of deleting them, so it runs
    with assertions on, and lays out both formats with 256-byte rows, which
    AMD's driver needs to import them (s30-0001, s30-0002).
  * Every shrink of a stereo eye, on either axis, is the average of the
    samples each pixel covers within its own eye; before, an eye shrunk by
    up to 2:1 was sampled bilinearly. An eye shown at its size or larger
    stays bilinear (s30-0003), and its bilinear samples no longer reach
    into the other eye at its inner edge (s30-0004).
  * X11 windows declare the size one view is shown at in
    _KDE_NET_WM_STEREO_CONTENT_ONE_VIEW_SIZE: the X11 window keeps the
    program's own size, its views at their own resolution, and its place
    on screen is one view at that size, so the two 1920x540 views of a
    half top-and-bottom 1920x1080 video, in a 3840x540 window, are shown
    at 1920x1080 with one resize. KWin announces support version 6
    (s30-0005).
  * STEREO3D.md describes both, and that each eye has its own resolution
    (s30-0006).
