kwin (4:6.7.4-2+stereo3d31) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Sparky-OS/kwin stereo3d31 is stereo3d30 plus three commits: the fix of
    a full-screen stereo window repainting the outputs next to it, the
    mirror that copies the composed eyes, and the release of the held eyes
    when the swapchains are replaced.
  * A full-screen full side by side window no longer damages the output
    next to it: SurfaceItem::addDamage took the whole buffer, both eyes
    wide, for the scale of a view, so the damage grew by a pixel into the
    neighbouring output, which then rendered a frame for every picture of
    the film while nothing on it changed. A view now scales with one eye's
    width (s31-0001, with testStereoOutputRepaint).
  * A mirror of a stereo output copies the two eyes the composing output
    composed instead of painting the scene again, and then runs its own
    output layout (frame packing, anaglyph, half side by side or top and
    bottom, rows, columns, checkerboard, the left eye on a 2D screen),
    scaled once into the place its view shows the scene. It paints the
    scene as before for a 2D source, another GPU, a rotated output,
    another colour space, an ICC profile, frame sequential, a stereo pair of
    outputs and the picture and depth layouts, and for every output with
    KWIN_STEREO_REPLICA_COPY=0 (s31-0002).
  * The eyes held for mirrors are let go when the swapchains are replaced
    (s31-0003).
