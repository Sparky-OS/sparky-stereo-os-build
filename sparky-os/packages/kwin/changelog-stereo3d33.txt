kwin (4:6.7.4-2+stereo3d33) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Sparky-OS/kwin stereo3d33 is stereo3d32 plus three commits from the
    A10's evidence (ORDER 230): the rest of the repaint of the output next to
    a full screen stereo window, one conversion table per pair of colour
    descriptions, and the mirror that converts the eyes it copies; and the
    four commits of the X11 one-view place contract (sView lane, s33-0004 to
    s33-0007).
  * A full-screen window drawn at another size than its buffer (a film larger
    than the output through a viewport, a fractional scale) no longer damages
    the output next to it: the padding added to a surface's damage for the
    filtering between pixels is cut at the surface (s33-0001, with the A10's
    cases in testStereoOutputRepaint: the 3D film on a laptop panel no longer
    makes the TV beside it render 20 frames a second).
  * ColorConversionTable (s33-0002): the conversion of a pair of colour
    descriptions (transfer function, matrix, tone mapping, clamp, encoding)
    is built once from KWin's colour pipeline, cached, and reused for every
    frame, both eyes and every output; it is rebuilt only when either
    description changes. GLShader::setColorspaceUniforms takes its values
    from it. The same table converts HDR content (PQ, BT.2020) into an SDR
    output with deep colour in floating point, so the output's depth is
    applied once. Test: testColorConversion.
  * A mirror of a stereo output whose colour space differs from the
    composing output's (brightness or dimming, another colour resolution)
    copies the composed eyes and converts them once, in the same draw that
    scales them, with the pair's table, when that gives what painting the
    scene again would give (within one level of 10 bits for SDR content);
    HDR content on screen that would differ, and an ICC profile, keep the old
    path with the reason logged; KWIN_STEREO_REPLICA_COPY=0 keeps the old
    path everywhere (s33-0003).
  * The X11 one-view place contract (s33-0004 to s33-0007): version 7 of the
    root property, serialled one-view size proposals held until the client
    answers and its frame arrives, move and stacking requests kept with a
    separate native raster, size hints refreshed into the pending place; with
    its tests (X11WindowTest).
