kwin (4:6.7.4-2+stereo3d50) unstable;

  * A popup larger than the room inside the invisible border of an output
    that shows 3D (a menu taller than the output leaves, an Open With or
    Create New with many entries) keeps its level: StereoDepth::level()
    flattened it to the screen's level as a popped thing touching the
    output's edge, so its sub-menu was drawn behind its parent (s50-0001,
    ORDER 464).
  * The launch feedback (the busy cursor) is part of the pointer on an
    output that shows 3D: the icon takes the pointer's plane, the bounce
    goes in depth only, between that plane and the popped limit above it,
    and the blinking and static feedback follow the plane; on an output that
    does not show 3D the bounce is KDE's own, up and down on the screen. It
    follows the output under the pointer (s50-0002, ORDER 464).
  * The windows beneath the active one step back one layer each, a layer
    being a fifth of the sunk limit, the first right behind the screen; from
    the sixth on they share the limit with the bottom of the pile and never
    go behind the wallpaper. Before, they shared the range among them, so
    the one window beneath the active one was at the limit, the wallpaper's
    depth (s50-0003, ORDER 464).
  * A chain of menus stacks one popped limit per level: level n is n limits
    from its base, the screen for a plain window and the pinned window's
    level for a window kept above the others; a dialog is a level of the chain; a menu alone stays at the
    limit and opening a sub-menu moves nothing above it (before, the chain
    shared the limit and a parent moved back). Notifications and on-screen
    displays are unchanged (s50-0004, ORDER 464).
  * The active output starts, and comes back when the active output goes
    away, on the screen marked main in Display Settings (priority 1), not on
    the first output the backend lists; the pointer starts on its centre
    (s50-0005, ORDER 464). Tests: testStereoDepthStack,
    testStartupFeedbackStereo and testOutputOrder (s50-0006 to s50-0008);
    testStereoDepthPopups, testStereoDepthPinnedStack, testStereoDepthPinned,
    testStereoDepthMotion and testQuickStereo expect the new levels.
