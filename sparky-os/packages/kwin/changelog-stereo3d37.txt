kwin (4:6.7.4-2+stereo3d37) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Sparky-OS/kwin stereo3d37 is stereo3d36 plus the five commits of
    ORDER 291 (s37-0001 to s37-0005).
  * The wallpaper keeps its size and is cut at the sides (s37-0002): the
    wallpaper lies at the sunk limit, behind every window, and its copy
    moves in each eye. The desktop window is no longer drawn wider to reach
    both edges, which stretched each eye (by 0.625 % at 1280 wide with the
    default Background depth) and scaled a stereo wallpaper's own
    disparity. The columns either eye's copy leaves open are cut from both
    sides, the same columns in both eyes: half the level's parallax per
    side, rounded up, from the user's Background depth and each output's
    own width (5 columns at 1920 wide with the default of 10, 4 at 1280).
    The wallpaper's fill mode still lays the picture over the whole screen.
    With a Background depth of 0, and on 2D outputs, nothing changes.
    Test: testStereoDepthWallpaper.
  * The lock screen's wallpaper lies at the desktop's level (s37-0003): the
    surfaces the greeter places below its own, as the layer of a stereo
    video wallpaper is, are drawn at the sunk limit and cut as the
    desktop's are; the clock, the password field and the rest of the
    greeter stay at the screen. The depth picture of the L + depth outputs
    follows. Test: testStereoDepthWallpaper (lock screen cases).
  * A stereo capture through ScreenShot2 copies each eye as it is
    (s37-0001): where nothing covers the output (the frame is transparent
    black there, as in the columns cut from a wallpaper), the joined picture
    kept whatever its new memory held. Test:
    testScreenshotStereo::testUncoveredPixelsAreTransparentInBothEyes.
  * KWIN_VIRTUAL_OUTPUT_SIZES gives virtual outputs their own sizes for
    headless proofs (s37-0004); STEREO3D.md says where the wallpaper lies
    and what is cut (s37-0005).
