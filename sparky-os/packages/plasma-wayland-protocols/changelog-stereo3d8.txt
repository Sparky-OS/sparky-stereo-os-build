plasma-wayland-protocols (1.21.0-1+stereo3d8) unstable; urgency=medium

  * kde-stereo-content-v1 version 3 (s8-0002, f04d76a):
    kde_stereo_content_manager_v1.get_depth makes a kde_stereo_depth_v1
    for a surface. Its depth_state event says whether the elements inside
    the surface may be shown in depth now (the surface is on a stereo
    output and awake, not one of the windows lying flat behind the active
    one) and which of its edges touch its output's edge;
    set_desktop_depth marks the surface's pair as the desktop's own
    elements drawn once per eye rather than stereo content; set_place and
    unset_place declare where a popup surface sits, as a fraction of the
    user's limits. Nothing changes for clients of versions 1 and 2. The
    contract test checks the new wire values.
  * The protocol text is the one KWin and libstereo-declare carry, byte for
    byte (s8-0001, 2ee5d41); no wire change.
