libxext (2:1.3.4-1+stereo3d1) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Sparky Stereo OS: programs that open and close X displays from several
    threads no longer break their heap (queue row 762c). Since commit
    ed8d50ba the Generic Event extension frees its process-wide record when
    the last display using it is closed, while another thread may be
    writing a new display into that record (XextAddDisplay); glibc then
    aborts ("malloc(): unaligned tcache chunk detected") or the program
    crashes. DisplayXR's OpenXR runtime died this way at start. The record
    now lives as long as the process, as libXrandr keeps its own
    (libxext-xge-static-extension-record.patch, Origin: vendor; no fix was
    found in X.Org's libXext, Salsa's packaging or Debian on 2026-11-21).
    Nothing else in libXext changes. Retired when Debian ships a fix.
    Test: tests/xge-thread-stress (crashes on 2:1.3.4-1+b4, finishes with
    the fix).
  * Carry Helmut Grohne's patch from Debian bug 1024924, which gives the
    udeb the noudeb build profile: the edition builds no udeb.
  * Debian's source format 1.0 is kept: both patches are applied in the
    source and kept with their headers in debian/patches.
