plasma-union (6.8.80~git20261022.2163195c-0+stereo3d1) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Sparky Stereo OS: Union master 2163195c (2026-10-22) with Debian's
    plasma-union 6.7.4-2 packaging, plus the twelve commits of the
    edition's depth in Union (danielcamposramos/union stereo3d-depth,
    7c8ab92f), so an element's depth comes from its style and is shown on
    Stereo KWin, and a 2D output shows every element exactly as before:
  * properties and CSS (s1-0001, s1-0003): depth in the display group,
    read as depth: sunk | screen | popped | <percentage> | <number>, and
    inset box shadows.
  * Depth (s1-0002, s1-0009, s1-0010): a declared depth wins; without one,
    an outer shadow raises the element and an inset shadow sinks it, by the
    shadow's strength against the strongest in the window, and an element
    with no shadow lies on its parent. Menus, popups, tooltips and dialogs
    drawn inside a window use the user's limits (SunkLimit and PoppedLimit
    in kwinrc's [StereoDepth], 10 and 4 pixels at 1920 wide by default);
    a window's own elements use ElementLimit, 1 pixel by default, nested
    and fitted as Stereo KWin stacks windows and fits menus.
  * Outputs (s1-0004 to s1-0006, s1-0012): the Qt Quick output sets each
    item's _q_stereo_parallax and leaves the moves to Qt Quick while it
    draws the window once per eye; the Qt Widgets output gives widgets
    their parallax and moves the parts it draws itself.
  * Breeze (s1-0007): the depths its shadows do not say: checked buttons,
    the current tab and selected rows sink, menus, popups, tooltips and
    dialogs pop, a flat tool button rises under the pointer.
  * Plasma (s1-0008, s1-0011): Stereo KWin tells each window, through
    libstereo-declare 1.5, whether its elements may show depth now.
  * Build-Depends: libstereo-declare-dev (>= 1.5.0~) and
    qt6-base-private-dev for the exchange; the tests run in a D-Bus session
    as well as an X server, which the depth tests need for KConfig.
  * kde-style-union depends on plasma-version-base-6.7, the Plasma it is built
    against and tested with: dh_plasmaversionbase reads the release from the
    upstream version, which for this snapshot of master is a pre-release of
    6.9 with a suffix it cannot read.
