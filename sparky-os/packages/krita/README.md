# krita: the stereo and deep-colour Krita

Debian's krita 1:5.3.4.1+dfsg-1, whose tarball is upstream's krita-6.0.4.1 tree, built as Krita's Qt 6 flavour (Krita 6.0.4.1), with the canvas of a stereo document shown per eye and the deep canvas (queue row 148, Krita's milestone 1).
It succeeds the edition's published 1:6.0.4+dfsg-1+stereo3d7+lc1, built outside this tree in October, and takes the place of ORDER 46c's catalogue rebuild 1:6.0.4+dfsg-1+stereo3d8+lc1 (never published): the two of its sentences that remain are in this recipe's catalogue patch with 46c's words, and the other three belonged to the "Stereo 3D" setting this version replaces.

- **What it does:** a document whose file the stereo layer reads as a pair packed side by side or top and bottom (the file's own mark, such as PNG's `sTER` or JPS's descriptor, then the person's choice for that file, then the shape of an unmarked picture), or a document with the top-level layers "Left eye" and "Right eye", shows each eye its own picture while View > Enable 3D is on (the default, for the window and the session). A packed picture shows each eye's view in both halves, so the image keeps its size and coordinates and every tool works where it is used. Krita hands Stereo KWin the two pictures side by side, left eye first, declared once, from a layer below its window; guides, the selection, the brush outline and the other decorations stay on the screen plane, the same in both eyes. Where the pair cannot be shown (Krita on X11, OpenGL ES, a stereo surface the graphics stack refuses) the canvas shows the left eye and says why, on the canvas, in the log and in Enable 3D's tooltip; in wrap-around mode and on the QPainter canvas it shows the picture as it is and says so. View > 3D Input Format is the stereo layer's menu for the file's packing and eye order (the shared per-file store, never the file); View > Display Settings opens the display settings. A 2D document is drawn as before.
- **The deep canvas:** under a Wayland compositor Krita runs on the native Wayland platform (Xwayland has no 30-bit visual), its canvas surface is the deepest the stack offers (16 bits per channel by default, 10 and 8 selectable), and the canvas says when it reaches the screen with fewer bits per channel than the document has.
- **The patches** (`patches/`, written from the edition's fork by `export-patches.py`; the fork is `invent.kde.org/danielcamposramos/krita`, branch `order148b-canvas-per-eye` on upstream's tag v6.0.4.1): the eight October patches of +stereo3d7 (native Wayland, the 16-bit surface, the canvas layer, its colour description, the deep window, the resource cache's transaction), upstream's own SIP commits in place of our SIP patch, upstream's native Wayland toolbar fix, and the three commits of row 148. The catalogue patch `krita-order148b-canvas-per-eye.po.patch` (in `sparky-os/translations`, made by `make-po-patch.py`) carries every sentence the patches add in the nine languages besides English. Krita's README sets a moratorium on AI-assisted contributions, so nothing is offered upstream.
- **debian/:** Debian's packaging moved to Qt 6 and KDE Frameworks 6 (asserted line by line in `make-source.sh`), the Build-Depends `libstereo-layer-dev (>= 1.22.0~)`, and the Depends `libqt6widgets6 (>= 6.11.2+dfsg-5+stereo3d3)` (the edition's Qt keeps a translucent window's deep alpha), `libkf6kcmutils-bin` and `kscreen` (View > Display Settings runs `kcmshell6 kcm_kscreen`), and `qt6-wayland` (Qt's Wayland shell integration: without it Qt makes no Wayland window, and Krita runs on X11, flat and at 8 bits). KSeExpr has no Qt 6 build in Debian, so the SeExpr fill layer is not built, as in the earlier +stereo3d builds.
- **Build:** `build.sh WORK DEBIAN_SOURCE REPO [PREVIOUS] [IMAGE]`: `make-source.sh` makes the `+lc1` source, then `release/build-from-lc-dsc.sh` builds it. Every package it needs is published, so there are no siblings.
- **Debian's own Krita on the edition:** Debian's 1:5.3.4.1+dfsg-1+b2 needs MLT 7.42, newer than the edition's pinned MLT 7.40, so it does not install there; the edition's Krita builds against the edition's MLT.
