krita (1:6.0.4.1+dfsg-1+stereo3d1) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Sparky Stereo OS: Debian's 1:5.3.4.1+dfsg-1, whose tarball is upstream's
    krita-6.0.4.1 tree, built as Krita's Qt 6 flavour (Krita 6.0.4.1), as the
    edition's 1:6.0.4+dfsg-1+stereo3d7 was: on Qt 5 the canvas cannot pass
    8 bits per channel and has no surface of its own for a stereo pair.
    Debian's packaging moved to Qt 6 and KDE Frameworks 6 (KSeExpr has no
    Qt 6 build in Debian, so its fill layer is not built, as before).
  * The canvas of a stereo document per eye (queue row 148): a document whose
    file the stereo layer reads as a pair packed side by side or top and
    bottom (its mark, the person's choice for that file, then its shape), or a
    document with the top-level layers "Left eye" and "Right eye", shows each
    eye its own picture while View > Enable 3D is on (the default, for the
    window and the session). A packed picture shows each eye's view in both
    halves, so the image keeps its size and coordinates and every tool works
    where it is used; Stereo KWin gets the two pictures side by side, left eye
    first, declared once, from a layer below the window, and the decorations
    and the brush outline stay on the screen plane, the same in both eyes.
    Where the pair cannot be shown the canvas shows the left eye and says why;
    in wrap-around mode and on the QPainter canvas it shows the document as it
    is, and says why.
    View > 3D Input Format is the stereo layer's menu for the file's packing
    and eye order (kept in the shared per-file store, never in the file);
    View > Display Settings opens the display settings. This replaces the
    "Stereo 3D" setting of +stereo3d2 to +stereo3d7 (off by default, read at
    start-up, one half shown, "Paint on the Right View").
  * A 2D document is drawn as on a window without the layer: its canvas goes
    through a layer with one buffer, which keeps the depth of the canvas
    surface, with the decorations and the brush outline painted on that
    picture, so the outline is the inverse of the picture under it; the
    window paints its background before the separators of its docks. On
    NVIDIA's own OpenGL, which does not show the layer, no session on the
    layer starts: every canvas is drawn in the window, and a stereo document
    shows its left eye and says why.
  * The deep canvas: the canvas says when it reaches the screen with fewer
    bits per channel than the document has (10-bit surface, or half float for
    a 16-bit document), at every depth the standard defines.
  * Carried from upstream: "Update SIP ABI to 13.8" and "Make the selection
    of SIP ABI automatic" (Dmitry Kazakov, master, 30182dbf and bf7fa438),
    which replace our own SIP patch, and "Fix an assert when modifying
    toolbars on native Wayland" (Dmitry Kazakov, krita/6.0, cd944914).
  * Kept from +stereo3d7: the native Wayland platform under a Wayland
    compositor, the 16-bit canvas surface (the deepest by default), the canvas
    layer with its colour description, the deep window of a stereo session,
    and the resource cache's single transaction.
  * The edition's sentences in the ten languages
    (krita-order148b-canvas-per-eye.po.patch).
