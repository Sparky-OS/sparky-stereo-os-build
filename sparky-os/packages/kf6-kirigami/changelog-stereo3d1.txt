kf6-kirigami (6.32.0-1+stereo3d1) unstable; urgency=medium

  * The edition's build of Debian's 6.32.0-1 (queue row 77 milestone 2).
  * Icon: while the edition's Qt Quick draws a window once per eye, an icon the theme has in stereo
    shows each eye its view, asked for inside that eye's pass, with no animation; it marks its window
    so that the KDE platform theme draws it once per eye on an output that shows two eyes. Any other
    icon, and any window not drawn once per eye, is drawn as before (s1-0001).
  * Kirigami's Icon by Marco Martin, Aleix Pol Gonzalez and KDE; packaged in Debian by the Debian
    Qt/KDE Maintainers.
