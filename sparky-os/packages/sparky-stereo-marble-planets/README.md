The maps are the ones Marble offers in its Download Maps dialog (KDE's catalogue https://marble.kde.org/maps-v3.xml), listed with their SHA-256, licence and credit in maps.list: Mercury, Venus (two), Mars (four), the Moon (five, beside Debian's Clementine map), Europa and Ganymede, Dione, Mimas, Rhea and Titan, all in the public domain.

Dependencies:
-------------
marble-data

Build:
-------------
OUT=/path/to/output ./build.sh
