qt6-base (6.11.2+dfsg-5+stereo3d13) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Wayland: a child window with Qt::WindowStaysOnBottomHint lies below
    its parent (s13-0001, Sparky-OS/qtbase lock-wallpaper-below-291):
    its subsurface is placed below the parent's surface
    (wl_subsurface.place_below), and back above it when the hint is
    cleared; the window's _q_wayland_below_parent property says where it
    lies, so that a program can tell that this Qt does it. Without the
    hint nothing changes: a new subsurface stays on top of its parent, as
    the protocol says. The lock screen draws its wallpaper in such a
    window, below the prompt, so that Stereo KWin can place the wallpaper
    behind the screen and keep the prompt at it.
    Test: tst_surface::subsurfaceStaysOnBottom.
