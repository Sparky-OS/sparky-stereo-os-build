gtk4 (4.24.1+ds-1+stereo3d4) unstable; urgency=medium

  * GtkScrollbar: scroll arrows a theme can turn on (queue row 147, milestone 3, stereo3d-gtkscrollbar-steppers): GTK 3's four steppers come back as stepper nodes, a backward and a secondary forward one before the range, a secondary backward and a forward one after it, with GTK 3's names (.up moves the slider up or left, .down down or right). A stepper takes room only when the theme gives it a size, so a theme without stepper rules (GTK's own, Breeze, Adwaita) draws every scrollbar as before, and a theme shows the arrows of its placement, together at the end or apart at both ends, as in GTK 2 and GTK 3. As in GTK 3: the primary button moves by a step, the secondary one by a page, both going on while held (after GTK 3's 500 ms, through the range's own autoscroll), the middle button moves to the start or the end; a stepper is insensitive while the slider is at the end it moves towards, and in right-to-left text the horizontal range's flip is followed. The scrollbar lays out its range and steppers itself instead of a box layout, so a stepper without a size adds no spacing; it follows the range's adjustment also when it made one itself. Searched first (2026-11-27): GTK's main branch has no stepper since "range: Remove steppers" (7e525ca6, Timm Bäder, 2017), and no merge request or issue brings them back.
