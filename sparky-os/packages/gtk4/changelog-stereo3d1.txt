gtk4 (4.24.1+ds-1+stereo3d1) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Sparky Stereo OS: a stereo GtkGLArea (ORDER 137, stereo3d-gtkglarea-stereo).
    GtkGLArea draws into a framebuffer of its own and had no way to hand
    two views to the compositor. A stereo area (property stereo) emits its
    render signal twice for each frame, the left view first, each into a
    framebuffer of the area's size, and gtk_gl_area_get_current_eye() says
    which view the handler draws. The views are copied side by side into one
    opaque texture, twice the width of the area, which goes to the
    compositor on a subsurface of its own, the way GtkGraphicsOffload hands
    over a picture, declared as full side by side stereo content with
    kde_stereo_content_v1 (version 2 or later, so the area keeps its size
    on the screen). The rest of the window stays the window's own surface,
    drawn once and seen the same by both eyes.
  * An area that cannot be shown per eye says so (properties stereo-shown
    and stereo-error, one log message with the reason) and renders its left
    view alone as an area without stereo does; it never shows the two views
    side by side in its place. An area without stereo, and every other
    widget and window, is unchanged.
  * New API: GtkGLAreaEye, gtk_gl_area_set_stereo, gtk_gl_area_get_stereo,
    gtk_gl_area_get_stereo_shown, gtk_gl_area_get_stereo_error,
    gtk_gl_area_get_current_eye. New test: testsuite/gtk/glarea.
    Searched first: GTK's main branch, its merge requests and issues
    (2026-11-07) have no stereo, multiview or per-eye GL in GtkGLArea.
  * gdk: an EGL dma-buf export that leaves a plane without an open file
    descriptor is a failed export (stereo3d-gdk-dmabuf-export-open-fds).
    Mesa's zink on NVIDIA's proprietary Vulkan driver reports success and
    does not write the descriptors; GTK passed uninitialised integers to the
    compositor, which ended the connection (EBADF) as soon as a GtkGLArea
    inside a GtkGraphicsOffload was offloaded. GTK now draws the texture
    itself, as for any export that cannot be done.
