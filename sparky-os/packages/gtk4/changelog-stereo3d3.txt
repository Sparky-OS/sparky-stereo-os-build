gtk4 (4.24.1+ds-1+stereo3d3) unstable; urgency=medium

  * GtkGLArea: the views of a stereo area can have a size of their own (queue row 137, milestone 3, stereo3d-gtkglarea-stereo-view-size): gtk_gl_area_set_stereo_view_size() and the properties stereo-view-width and stereo-view-height. A film's views are rendered at the film's own size and the compositor does the one resize to where the area is shown; the viewport says the size of what a handler draws, per eye or the left view alone. An area that sets no size is as before.
  * GtkGLArea on X11: the views of a stereo area are drawn on their declared child window with OpenGL, a blit and a swap on an EGL or GLX surface of the window, instead of being read back and put through the X server (stereo3d-gtkglarea-stereo-x11-gl). For two 1920x1080 views a frame the area runs at about 58 frames a second for 2.8 ms of processor a frame against 18 at 19 ms on zink, and 60 at 1.7 ms against 26 to 33 at 16 ms on NVIDIA's own libraries; on a software renderer both ways run at 12 to 15. The readback stays for a display that gives the window no OpenGL surface, said in the log, and behind GDK_X11_STEREO_READBACK=1. The first frames are repeated until the window manager has taken the declaration, where it says so. An area a parent clips (a scrolled window) says that it cannot be a window of its own and draws its left view alone.
  * GtkGLArea: the format and the color state of the picture (stereo3d-gtkglarea-format-color-state): gtk_gl_area_set_format() takes 8 bits (the default), 10 bits, 16 bits (which carries 12), half float and single float; gtk_gl_area_set_color_state() takes sRGB (the default), Rec. 2100 PQ, Rec. 2100 linear, sRGB linear or any state built from CICP numbers such as hybrid log-gamma. The framebuffer, the texture and the dma-buf handed to the compositor have that format and color state, per view in a stereo area: XB24, XB30, AB48 and AB4H with an alpha of one; the views of a single float area are rendered in single floats and their picture is kept in half floats, the deepest float a dma-buf has. Where the driver exports no dma-buf of the format the area says why and draws its left view alone.
  * gdk/wayland: the RGB pictures of subsurfaces are described to the compositor (wp_color_manager_v1), and sRGB is described with gamma 2.2 where the compositor lists that and not the named sRGB transfer function, which version 2 of the protocol deprecates and KWin does not list (stereo3d-gdk-wayland-subsurface-color-description). Before, GTK used no color management on KWin at all and a picture in Rec. 2100 PQ could not be given a subsurface. GTK's own windows and YUV pictures keep upstream's GDK_DEBUG=color-mgmt; GDK_WAYLAND_DISABLE=wp_color_manager_v1 turns the descriptions off.
  * New API: gtk_gl_area_set_stereo_view_size, gtk_gl_area_get_stereo_view_size, gtk_gl_area_set_format, gtk_gl_area_get_format, gtk_gl_area_set_color_state, gtk_gl_area_get_color_state. testsuite/gtk/glarea gains their tests. Searched first (2026-11-25): GTK's main branch, merge requests and issues have no depth or color state for GtkGLArea and no view size.
