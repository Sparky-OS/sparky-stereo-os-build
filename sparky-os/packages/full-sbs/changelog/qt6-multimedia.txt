qt6-multimedia (6.11.2-3+stereo3d7) unstable; urgency=medium

  * The VideoOutput stereo layer hands the compositor each view at the
    resolution the frame carries it (patch 0011, edition only): a half
    top-and-bottom 1920x1080 video is one 3840x540 buffer, and Stereo KWin
    resizes once. Before, the layer restored half views to full size with
    stereoScaling on the CPU and the compositor resized them again;
    stereoScaling stays for a view shown flat in the scene graph.
