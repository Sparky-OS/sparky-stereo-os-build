mpv-dmo (1:0.41.0-dmo9+stereo3d4) unstable; urgency=medium

  * A 3D video's views go to the compositor at the resolution the video has
    them, and Stereo KWin resizes them once (patches 0052, 0053): the video
    layer of vo_gpu and vo_gpu_next measures the window in the view's own
    samples and each view is drawn into its half without a resize of ours.
    A half top-and-bottom 1920x1080 video goes as one 3840x540 layer to a
    full screen window; before, each view was stretched to 1920x1080 here
    and resized again by the compositor.
  * The autostereo filter tells the format filter the view's display aspect
    with all its digits, so a window of the video's own shape gets a
    destination of its exact size instead of one row less.
