qt6-3d (6.11.2+dfsg-2+stereo3d1) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Sparky Stereo OS: the OpenGL renderer's context follows the format
    its Qt3DWindow asks for, so a window given a stereo format after it
    is made (the stereo fork of KDAB's Stereo Demonstrator, ORDER 241)
    gets a stereo context on X11 too, where the drawable takes the
    context's configuration (stereo3d-renderer-context-follows-surface-
    format). The RHI renderer and windows without a format of their own
    are unchanged.
