plasma-gamemode (0.0~git20240315.4d60358-2+stereo3d1) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Sparky Stereo OS: the Game Mode applet no longer takes plasmashell down
    at login (ORDER 755). GamesModel::reload() ran twice when the GameMode
    service appeared while the first ListGames answer was on its way, and
    the first answer's handler deleted the second reload's watcher and set
    the member to null, so the second handler called deleteLater() on a
    null pointer (SIGSEGV in libplasma-gamemode.so). Each handler now owns
    its watcher and the member is cleared only by the watcher it points to
    (0001-gamesmodel-each-answer-owns-its-watcher.patch). Nothing was found
    upstream or in Debian's tracker on 2026-11-19; the applet and its
    behaviour are unchanged.
