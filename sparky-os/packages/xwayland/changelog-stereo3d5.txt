xwayland (2:24.1.13-1+stereo3d5) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Without glamor (a machine or a virtual machine with no 3D acceleration,
    or XWAYLAND_NO_GLAMOR=1), a window declared full side-by-side stereo with
    version 4 of the KDE declaration is a pair again. The pixmap its program
    presents, twice the window's width, gets shared memory and a wl_buffer as
    a pixmap of a toplevel's size does, so Present flips it to the window's
    surface whole (0010). Before, it was copied into the one eye wide window,
    which keeps the left eye: an OpenGL quad buffer program showed half of
    its left eye to each eye, and a stereo child window with its own surface
    showed a right eye that never changed (ORDER 560).
  * The damage of such a flip covers both eyes: a compositor reads only the
    damaged part of a wl_shm buffer, and the right eye kept an old frame
    (0011).
  * Test, in the source's own xwayland suite: stereo-present-pair on Xwayland
    without glamor under a headless weston, for a toplevel and for a child
    window, with an undeclared window as the control (0012). It fails on
    +stereo3d4. The build runs it: weston, libxcb-present-dev,
    libxcb-composite0-dev and libxcb1-dev join the build dependencies.
