xwayland (2:24.1.15-1+stereo3d4) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Xwayland no longer dies of the compositor's "explicit sync is used, but
    no acquire point is set" (wp_linux_drm_syncobj_surface_v1 error 4), which
    closed every X11 program with it. After a program presented with explicit
    sync, an update of its window that fell back to implicit sync (radeonsi
    and every other Mesa driver) kept the window's syncobj surface and
    attached the program's own flipped buffer with no acquire point; one way
    there is a window update queued while the surface moved off a child
    window and back to it (queue row 865). Carried from upstream's merge
    request !2399, "xwayland: drop surface sync on implicit sync fallback" by
    Thomas H.P. Andersen (0015).
