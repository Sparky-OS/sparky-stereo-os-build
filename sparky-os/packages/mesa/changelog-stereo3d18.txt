mesa (26.1.6-1+stereo3d18) unstable; urgency=medium

  * GLX takes zink through DRI3, not kopper, while the compositor announces
    stereo content (0029): kopper has no second view of a window. The session
    no longer needs LIBGL_KOPPER_DISABLE=1 for an X11 program to get both
    views; LIBGL_KOPPER_DISABLE=0 or 1 still decides when set, and the route
    is named in the log under LIBGL_DEBUG=verbose.
  * eglExportDMABUFImageMESA gives a real dma-buf for a zink image on NVIDIA's
    Vulkan driver, and says so when a driver gives none (0030, 0031; ORDER
    457). zink made the image of an export with the linear modifier alone,
    which NVIDIA lists without the colour attachment blend feature, so the
    export failed and the call returned success with no descriptor; GTK then
    sent uninitialised integers to the compositor and the program lost its
    connection. The image takes a modifier the driver can render with, and a
    failed query returns EGL_FALSE with EGL_BAD_MATCH.
