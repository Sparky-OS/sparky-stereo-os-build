mesa (26.1.6-1+stereo3d28) forky; urgency=medium

  * A stereo child window of a window that is not on the screen yet is
    declared once the window is viewable (ORDER 50c, patch 0045). Tk creates
    the GL child of Netgen's drawing area and draws once while its top level
    is unmapped, and maps the top level about 0.7 s later. Stereo KWin can only
    take a child of a window that is on the screen, so the declaration made at
    that first swap met the wait of patch 0043 without an answer, the presents
    that completed as copies took the declaration back, and the child stayed
    flat after the window was mapped. Both GLX paths, DRI3 and software, now
    leave the declaration to the first swap after the window and every
    ancestor are mapped (map_state IsViewable); a top-level window and a child
    of a window mapped from the start are declared as before, and a child that
    is viewable and not taken is still told as before ("is shown flat").
  * A stereo child whose window is unmapped or minimised and comes back is a
    pair again (ORDER 50c, patch 0046). The presents a child makes while its
    window is away complete as copies and took the declaration back, and a
    flat child never flips, so it stayed flat after the window returned. A
    present that completes as a copy while the window is not viewable is left
    out of the pair rule, and the first completion after the window is viewable
    again declares the child anew. Both GLX paths.
  * libudev-dev and libxcb-keysyms1-dev are declared build dependencies, on
    every architecture (ORDER 50c). Debian's source lists neither, so meson
    found them only when another build dependency installed them: the amd64
    build of +stereo3d26 had them and its i386 twin did not, and a build on a
    later day lost them without a message. libudev lets the Vulkan drivers'
    VK_KHR_display notice display hotplug through a udev monitor; libxcb-keysyms
    gives the X11 Vulkan presentation the F1 capture hotkey. Both link into every
    libvulkan_*.so of mesa-vulkan-drivers, so its Depends names libudev1 and
    libxcb-keysyms1 on i386 too.
