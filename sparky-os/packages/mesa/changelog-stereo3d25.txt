  * The Vulkan stereo layer gives an X11 window its pair where the X server
    has no DRI3, as with Xwayland without glamor (ORDER 623): the driver's
    presentation puts its picture into the window there, so the window is
    declared for Stereo KWin to make it two views wide, and the driver's
    swapchain of both views is made once it is (at most a second,
    MESA_STEREO_DOUBLING_WAIT_MS); the program keeps working in one view's
    size. Before, the layer refused the two-view swapchain and the program
    ended. Mesa's own X11 presentation does the same for lavapipe, whose
    first present was out of date.
  * Where the window cannot be made two views wide (a child window, an
    override-redirect one, an older Stereo KWin) the layer offers one image
    layer, and a program that asks for two anyway is refused and the person
    is told through sparky-stereo-problems (mesa-vulkan-layer-stereo,
    one-view:vulkan:DEVICE) in the ten languages; a pair of that device
    shown later ends it.
