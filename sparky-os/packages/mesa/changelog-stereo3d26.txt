mesa (26.1.6-1+stereo3d26) forky; urgency=medium

  * A stereo window that is a child of another window shows its first pair
    (ORDER 17). The child is declared at the program's first swap and
    presented at once; Stereo KWin holds it some tens of milliseconds later
    (measured: a declaration 30 ms before the first swap is shown flat, 80 ms
    before it is a pair), so the first presents completed as copies and took
    the declaration back, and a flat child never flips, so it never returned:
    a program that draws once stayed flat until it made a new drawable. From
    KWin's declaration support 11, which writes _KDE_NET_WM_STEREO_DEPTH_LIMITS
    on a declared child once it holds the child, the first present of a
    declared child waits for the property, up to half a second, on DRI3 and
    on the software path (patch 0043). A compositor that announces less, and
    a window that is not a child, are not waited for; a compositor that does
    not answer in half a second is said on stderr and the presents go as
    before.
