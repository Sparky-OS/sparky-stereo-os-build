# Sparky Stereo Oxygen

Background artwork: The Oxygen Project, as credited by Oxygen's own metadata.json.
Source: installed `/usr/share/plasma/desktoptheme/oxygen`, metadata version 6.26.0.
Licence: GPL, exactly as declared in that metadata; it does not specify a version.
GPL version 2 and version 3 texts accompany this study for reference.
The original source remains available in KDE's Oxygen project: https://invent.kde.org/plasma/oxygen.

Modifications, 2026: Daniel Ramos and EchoSystems AI studios.
Only background frame surfaces are derived here.
The named frame slices have 91 percent opacity; centre gradients are made opaque before applying that alpha, preserving their RGB stops.
Masks and shadows retain the source geometry and alpha.
Plasma's foreground artwork and controls come from the installed Oxygen fallback theme.
No icons or other local theme assets are copied into this package.
The generator and per-file source hashes are in `tools/derive_oxygen.py` and `study/oxygen-derivation.json` in the corresponding source bundle.
