gimp (3.2.8-1+stereo3d1) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Sparky Stereo OS: the canvas of a stereo image per eye (queue row 137
    with row 148, the stereo GIMP's milestone 1; stereo3d-canvas-per-eye).
    A stereo image (top-level layers "Left eye" and "Right eye", as the
    stereo pictures plug-in opens MPO, JPS and PNS files, or a side-by-side
    or top-and-bottom picture in one of the plug-in's editing modes) is
    drawn once for each eye, each from its own content at the canvas's own
    size, while View > Enable 3D is on (on by default); the two pictures go
    to the compositor through the stereo GtkGLArea of the edition's GTK 3,
    side by side, left first, declared once, and the compositor does the
    one resize. Guides, the selection, paths and the tools' outlines stay
    over the picture at the screen, the same in both eyes. Where the canvas
    cannot be shown per eye the left eye is shown and the status bar says
    why. View > Display Settings opens the display settings, where each
    screen's 3D mode is chosen. A 2D image is drawn as before.
  * The strings in nine languages besides English
    (stereo3d-canvas-per-eye-po).
  * debian/control: Build-Depends libgtk-3-dev (>= 3.24.52-3+stereo3d1~)
    and libepoxy-dev for the stereo GtkGLArea; gimp Depends on
    kde-cli-tools and kscreen for View > Display Settings.
