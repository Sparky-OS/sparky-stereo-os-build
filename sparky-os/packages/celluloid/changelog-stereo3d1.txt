celluloid (0.29-3+stereo3d1) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Sparky Stereo OS: 3D films in 3D (queue row 137, stereo3d-3d-films).
    The video area is a stereo GtkGLArea for a film whose frames hold two
    views: each view is rendered once from libmpv (MPV_RENDER_PARAM_STEREO_VIEW,
    mpv-dmo +stereo3d12) at the size libmpv gives for it, and the area
    declares the pair as full side by side stereo content, on Wayland and on
    X11 (gtk4 +stereo3d3). Nothing is resized before the compositor. A 2D
    film is rendered as before, one picture, equal in both eyes.
  * The menu's 3D options are the edition's and no others: Enable 3D, 3D
    Input Format (the stereo layer's list, in its words), Swap left and right
    eyes, Save the correction into the file, Display Settings. The input
    format is offered for every film with a picture, shown in 3D or flat, so
    that a film that declares nothing can be given its packing; the swap and
    the save are offered only while the film is shown in 3D.
  * A correction is kept beside the film's resume position (mpv's watch-later
    entry) and the film's file is written only when the person chooses the
    save (libstereo-av: Matroska StereoMode with mkvpropedit, MP4 st3d, read
    back); after a save the entry's correction is cleared and the option is
    unmarked. A refused save says why and keeps the correction.
  * A film that cannot be shown per eye is shown flat, its left view alone,
    and the person is told once what failed and why: a window drawn by the
    processor on Wayland is said as such, every other cause points to the
    log, which keeps GTK's exact reason. A film without an audio track says
    "This film has no sound.". The window fits one view of a pair.
  * The new sentences are in the nine catalogues of the edition's languages
    (de, es, fr, ja, ko, pl, pt_BR, zh_CN, zh_TW); English is the source.
  * Depends on libmpv2 (>= 1:0.41.0-dmo9+stereo3d12~), which brings the
    render parameter, mkvtoolnix (the save into Matroska), kde-cli-tools and
    kscreen (Display Settings); builds with libstereo-policy-dev and
    libstereo-av-dev.
