qemu (1:11.1.2+ds-3+stereo3d8) unstable; urgency=medium

  * A guest in an HDMI 3D "L + depth" or "L + depth + graphics +
    graphics-depth" mode (HDMI 1.4a Appendix H, 3D_Structure_X 0100 and
    0101) is shown as two views. virtio-gpu reads the graphics and graphics
    depth rectangles of the stereo scanout command (104 bytes; a guest that
    sends 88 still works, a second layout without its graphics rectangles is
    refused with a guest error) (0017); the EDID declares both structures
    at 1080p60 (0018); the GTK display on Wayland draws the second view from
    the picture and its depth through libstereo-dibr and hands the
    compositor full side by side, declared once, each view at the picture's
    size (0019, 0020). Before, the EDID listed neither structure, and the
    viewer took the second picture of such a scanout for the right eye.
  * A stereo declaration the compositor refuses is said once and the stereo
    surface is hidden, instead of a flat, squeezed pair (0021).
  * The five Sparky-OS/qemu commits the depth work stands on are carried
    with it, so the stereo files equal the fork's order156-depth branch:
    line-alternative scanouts keep their metadata and their native line
    samples are copied (0012 to 0014), a rutabaga scanout disable clears
    the stereo metadata (0015), and the GL readback allocation is bounded
    (0016).
  * Build-Depends on libstereo-dibr-dev (>= 1.17.1) [amd64] in the control
    file and its template; the runtime dependency on libstereo-dibr1 comes
    from the library's shlibs file.
