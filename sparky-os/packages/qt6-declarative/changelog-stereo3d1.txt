qt6-declarative (6.11.2+dfsg-3+stereo3d1) unstable; urgency=medium

  * Sparky Stereo OS: a Qt Quick window whose style shows its elements in
    depth (Union on Stereo KWin) is drawn once per eye by Qt Quick itself,
    so Stereo KWin shows depth inside it; every other window is drawn as
    before (Sparky-OS/qtdeclarative stereo3d-6.11-depth, on v6.11.2 with
    Debian's one patch).
  * s1-0001 (6f4e119): an item's clip node follows a changed clip
    rectangle.
  * s1-0002 (5c961eb): while a window's _q_stereo_depth is set and the
    platform gives it two views, both render loops polish, sync and render
    the left eye, end that frame without presenting it, then polish, sync
    and render the right eye into the right buffer and present. Each item
    with a _q_stereo_parallax moves by its eye share relative to its
    nearest ancestor with one, a deeper item seen through its own
    rectangle; only node matrices and clip nodes change, so geometry,
    input and mapToScene stay 2D. Without depth one pass per frame, as
    before. With tst_qquickwindowstereo.
